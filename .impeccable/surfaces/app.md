# FINN app (Android): all screens

**Scope:** the whole native app: Brief, Watch, Ask (chats, tool steps, replies, reactions, sessions), company, brain + search + page reader, story, onboarding. **Mode:** Operate.

**Audience and job:** a professional investor on a phone between meetings in daylight: learn what changed in their companies, ask why, and have FINN watch or research. **Constraints:** three tabs (Brief · Watch · Ask) kept, Material navigation + system Back, 48dp targets, no invented numbers, no buy/sell advice. Replaces the generic dark-AI look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: FINN is built like Mumbai's money district: importance stands in setbacks. It refuses the category default of dark terminal + neon accent and the broker-app card stack.

OWN-WORLD: Sea-green stucco shell (app bars, nav indicator, the reversed plate), pale stone ground, lacquer-black ink, brass for FINN acting (working, tool steps, its reactions), deep red only for losses. Condensed deco caps (Josefin Sans) at exactly three steps: wordmark/screen title, ticker, lead headline; Public Sans for everything else, tabular figures. Square corners except Material's own pills.

STORY: The investor sees how much FINN read and how little deserves them, reads the one reversed story, scans ranked rows, then asks or opens a company brain; FINN's activity is always visible in brass.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sea-green top bar with the FINN wordmark and window chips; a count line; one reversed sea-green plate with a stepped crown for the most material story (ticker, NSE ref, headline, one-line why); rows each with a 1-3 step setback mark, ticker caps, headline, ref line; Material nav bar with a sea-green indicator.

FORM: Bombay Art Deco (Marine Drive / Fort), candidate 4 of 7; seed:ba5e5815265ed536. Signature move: the setback rank mark and stepped plate crown. Raises: one reversed plate per screen; exchange refs as catalogue numbers; fixed holding furniture; one display face at three steps; no ornament beyond the setback.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
