# ETERNAL — promised vs delivered

**Mixed credibility.** Where near-term claims could be checked—Blinkit margin direction, per-store NOV, working capital, NAOV stability—management delivered. Big-ticket guidance (FY29 $1bn EBITDA, District FY30, 6% margins) remains untested, and District breakeven and food-delivery growth stopped being disclosed.
