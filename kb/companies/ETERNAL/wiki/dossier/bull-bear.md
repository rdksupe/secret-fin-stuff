# ETERNAL — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Scale compounding fast: FY26 total income INR 55,760 crore vs INR 21,320 crore FY25; Q2 FY27 revenue INR 24,138 crore, still +77.6% YoY. (annual-report-2026 p.278; dossier/trajectory)
- Profitability inflecting: EBIT positive three straight quarters (INR 18→48→127 crore); EBITDA margin up 1.2%→3.1% in five quarters; Q2 FY27 PAT +92.3% YoY. (dossier/trajectory Q4 FY26–Q2 FY27)
- Near-term promises delivered: Blinkit margins expanded, NOV/store +16-17% to INR 9 lakh/day, NWC 14 days vs 18 guided; >70% mature-city NOV EBITDA positive. (guidance-ledger; transcripts/Q2-FY27-concall p.9,11-12)

## Bear

- FY26 consolidated profit fell to INR 366 crore from INR 527 crore despite revenue ~2.6x; Q2 FY27 PAT margin ~0.5% (INR 125 crore on INR 24,138 crore). (annual-report-2026 p.220; dossier/trajectory)
- Growth decelerating sharply: revenue YoY 201.9%→77.6% in four quarters; PAT (INR 92, 125 crore) still below Q4 FY26's INR 174 crore. (dossier/trajectory)
- Food delivery's INR 2,079 crore result carries the group: Going Out lost INR 292 crore, Hyperpure earned INR 16 crore; District losses and food-delivery growth disclosure stopped. (annual-report-2026 p.279-280; guidance-ledger)

## Watch next quarter

- Q3 FY27 print: does Blinkit NOV growth accelerate from 79% as promised for the December quarter, with no short-term margin hit?
- Whether District loss figures and food-delivery YoY growth disclosures resume, given breakeven was promised within 4-6 quarters from Q3 FY26.
- Store-count trajectory vs 3,500-4,000 target (currently +~50% YoY vs 100% growth promise) and NWC path from 14 to 12 days.
