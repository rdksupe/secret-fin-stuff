# ETERNAL — what the business is

Eternal Limited (formerly Zomato Limited) is one of the first home-grown new-age tech companies listed in India, operating through four key business segments: food delivery, quick commerce, going-out, and B2B supplies. Its platforms connect end users, restaurant partners, and delivery partners, and include the Blinkit quick-commerce platform, Hyperpure B2B supplies, and dining-out/entertainment ticketing services.

**Geographies:** Operations are overwhelmingly India-based; revenue from geographies outside India was INR 65 crore in FY26 (FY25: INR 51 crore), with non-current assets outside India of INR 4 crore. Subsidiaries are incorporated in India, Dubai, Ireland, and Sri Lanka.

- Consolidated total income of INR 55,760 crore in FY26 (FY25: INR 21,320 crore)
- Consolidated profit for the year of INR 366 crore in FY26 (FY25: INR 527 crore); standalone profit INR 2,655 crore
- Consolidated revenue from operations INR 54,364 crore (services INR 18,809 crore; goods INR 35,555 crore)
- Total segment results INR 2,027 crore in FY26 (FY25: INR 1,435 crore)
- No single customer represented 10% or more of total revenue in FY26
