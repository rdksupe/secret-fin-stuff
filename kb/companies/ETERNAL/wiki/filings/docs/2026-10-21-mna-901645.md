# **Sub.: Outcome of the Board Meeting held on October 21, 2026**

Dear Sir/ Ma'am,

Pursuant to Regulation 30, 33 and other applicable provisions of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 ("**Listing Regulations**") read with circulars issued by SEBI ("**SEBI Circular**"), we wish to inform you that the board of directors of Eternal Limited (formerly known as Zomato Limited) ("**Board**" and "**Company**", respectively) at its meeting held today i.e. October 21, 2026, inter alia, has considered and approved:

1. Unaudited financial results (standalone and consolidated) for the quarter and half year ended on September 30, 2026 ("**Financial Results**"). Further, the said results have been subjected to limited review by M/s Deloitte Haskins & Sells, statutory auditors of the Company. A copy of Financial Results, along with the limited review report issued by the statutory auditors, are enclosed.
2. Business Transfer Agreement ("**BTA**") between the Company and Zomato Entertainment Private Limited ("**ZEPL**"), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, to transfer the Going-out India business housed in the Company, along with its respective assets and liabilities (collectively referred to as "**Identified Business**") to ZEPL, by way of slump sale, on a going concern basis. The details as required under the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Circular is enclosed as **Annexure – A**.

Subsequently, the Going-out India business housed in two other wholly owned subsidiaries of the Company – Orbgen Technologies Private Limited ("**OTPL**") and Wasteland Entertainment Private Limited ("**WEPL**"), will also be consolidated into ZEPL. In order to enable this, the board of directors of ZEPL, OTPL and WEPL, have also granted in-principle approval at their respective board meetings held on October 21, 2026, to evaluate the means and manner of implementing the aforesaid business consolidation.

The BTA and aforesaid consolidation is being undertaken as part of an overall internal restructuring exercise to consolidate the Going-out India business operating across various group entities, in a single wholly owned subsidiary – ZEPL to streamline the group's corporate structure and achieve operational synergies.

3. Sale of 11,214 (Eleven thousand two hundred fourteen) Series B compulsorily convertible preference shares and 10 (Ten) equity shares in Adonmo Private Limited ("**Adonmo**") to Vivish Technologies Private Limited ("**Mygate**") and acquisition of 1,84,091 (One lakh eighty four thousand ninety one) Series C1 compulsorily convertible preference shares and 164 (One hundred sixty four) equity shares in Mygate.

{1}------------------------------------------------

The sale and acquisition of shares mentioned above is part of a single transaction (“**Transaction**”) wherein the Company will transfer all of its shares in Adonmo to Mygate in lieu of Mygate shares as consideration.

The Transaction is a step towards a merger proposed to be executed by Adonmo and Mygate (“**Proposed Merger**”). Post completion of the Proposed Merger, all other shareholders of Adonmo will become shareholders of the merged Mygate entity and Adonmo will cease to exist as a separate entity.

The Company will eventually own ~ 4.4% equity stake on a fully diluted basis in the merged Mygate entity. This stake is currently valued at INR 156 crore. The Company had invested INR 112 crore in Adonmo in January 2022.

Adonmo is one of India’s leading ambient digital advertising platforms, operating a cloud-connected network of screens across residential communities. Mygate is a technology platform focused on improving the living experience within residential gated communities by offering visitor management, security, accounting, and community engagement for residents.

The Transaction is subject to execution of definitive agreements, fulfilment of certain customary closing conditions precedent and other terms and conditions under the transaction documents. The details as required in respect of sale and acquisition of shares, under the Listing Regulations read with SEBI Circular are enclosed as **Annexure – B1** and **Annexure – B2** respectively.

The meeting of the Board commenced at 1:00 P.M. and concluded at 3:10 P.M.

The above information will also be hosted on the website of the Company i.e. [www.eternal.com](http://www.eternal.com).
