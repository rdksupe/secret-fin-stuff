# ITC — bull vs bear

## Bull

- FY2026 continuing operations remained highly profitable: consolidated PBT ₹28,033.39cr and standalone PBT ₹26,767.60cr. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p258, p162)
- FY2026 operating cash flow ₹18,464.31cr covered capex ₹2,182.89cr about 8.5x, indicating strong internal funding capacity. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p258)
- Revenue momentum is strong: quarter ended 2026-06-30 revenue ₹29,523.30cr, +27.6% YoY, after 2026-03-31 ₹23,821.48cr, +16.9% YoY. (quarterly_actuals 2026-06-30; 2026-03-31)

## Bear

- Latest quarter shows severe compression: EBITDA margin 17.3% versus 29.2%, op margin 15.8% versus 27.3%; PAT fell 16.2% despite revenue +27.6%. (quarterly_actuals 2026-06-30 vs 2025-06-30)
- Reported PAT is noisy: FY2025 included ₹15,583.12cr demerger gain; 2025-03-31 PAT ₹19,727.37cr was followed by 2026-03-31 PAT -72.7% YoY. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p258; quarterly_actuals 2025-03-31/2026-03-31)
- Segment revenue shares and geographic breakdown are not stated, so concentration across cigarettes, foods, paperboards and agri cannot be quantified from the dossier. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p10-11)

## Watch next quarter

- Whether EBITDA/op margins recover from 17.3%/15.8% toward prior 29-31% levels.
- Whether revenue growth above 20% sustains while PAT stops declining.
- Any disclosure of segment shares, geographic mix, or demerger-adjusted comparables.
