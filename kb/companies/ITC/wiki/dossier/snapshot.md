# ITC — what the business is

ITC Limited is a public limited company domiciled in India, listed on the NSE and BSE, and is a diversified conglomerate. Its businesses span Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (Cigarettes & Cigars, Foods, Personal Care Products, Education & Stationery Products, Safety Matches and Agarbattis), Paperboards, Paper and Packaging, and Agri Business.
