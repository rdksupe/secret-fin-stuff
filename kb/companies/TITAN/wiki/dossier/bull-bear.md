# TITAN — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Consolidated revenue crossed ₹76,000 crore ex-bullion, +28%; Q1 FY27 revenue +29.3% YoY and PAT +62.9%, showing momentum. (annual-report-2026 p153; quarterly_actuals 2026-06-30)
- Jewellery grew 33% with 27% same-store growth; Q3 revenue +43.3% and Q4 +80.5% with Damas consolidated. (annual-report-2026 p154-155; quarterly_actuals 2025-12-31, 2026-03-31)
- Q1 FY27 margins improved to 13.5% EBITDA and 12.3% operating; ₹407 crore customs-duty flow-through is pending for Q2-Q3. (quarterly_actuals 2026-06-30; guidance-ledger)

## Bear

- Q4 FY26 margin compressed to 7.2% EBITDA and 6.3% operating despite +80.5% revenue, showing mix can dilute profitability. (quarterly_actuals 2026-03-31)
- Jewellery margin promise was only partly kept: Q3 fell ~50bps YoY and Q1 FY27 normalized 10.9%, near not clearly above 11%. (guidance-ledger)
- CaratLane stayed below low double-digit EBIT margin (8.4% Q4, 9.6% Q1 FY27); international/Damas posted ₹82 crore Q4 loss. (guidance-ledger)

## Watch next quarter

- Q2-Q3 FY27 flow-through of ₹407 crore customs-duty gains and whether jewellery margin holds above 11%.
- Damas integration losses and GCC geopolitical impact on international jewellery.
- Whether Q1 FY27 >30% LFL/ticket-size benefit sustains; Tanishq additions and beYon store count are not stated.
