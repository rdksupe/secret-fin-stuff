# TITAN — what the business is

Titan Company Limited is a lifestyle company (part of the Tata Group ecosystem per the MD's remarks) that designs, manufactures and retails watches, jewellery and eyewear through large retail networks, and also operates a B2B engineering and automation subsidiary (TEAL). In FY2025-26 it crossed ₹75,000+ crore consolidated revenue (excluding bullion), delivering 28% revenue growth.

| Segment | Share | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Jewellery | not stated | Largest revenue line: manufactured jewellery ₹52,405 crore + traded ₹8,529 crore (p328); grew 33% with 27% same-store growth; brands include Tanishq (528 stores), Mia (₹2,000+ crore, 292 stores), CaratLane (378 stores, >30% growth), Zoya (13 boutiques); launched LGD brand 'beYon'; acquired 67% of Damas Jewellery (p153-156) |
| Watches | not stated | 17% NSV growth; analog watches grew 24% vs market 18%, ~27% analog market share; 1,292 retail stores (743 Titan World, 288 Helios, 252 Fastrack, 9 Helios Luxe) (p154) |
| EyeCare | not stated | Returned to double-digit growth in H2 FY2025-26; 'Vision meets Fashion' strategy (p153) |
| Emerging businesses | not stated | Fragrances & Women's Bags performed well; Indian Dress Wear muted (p153) |
| TEAL (B2B Engineering) | not stated | Wholly owned subsidiary; one of its best performances, driven by automation solutions for electronics manufacturing (p153) |

**Geographies:** Primarily India; international jewellery business expanding in North America (CaratLane stores in New Jersey and Dallas) with GCC presence impacted by geopolitical developments in March (p153, p156).

- Total income (standalone) ₹78,089 crore; Profit After Tax ₹4,630 crore; EBIT ₹7,242 crore at 9.3% margin (p14)
- Consolidated revenue crossed ₹76,000+ crore (excluding bullion), 28% growth (p153)
- 3,603 stores across 46 lakh sq. ft. retail area; 11 manufacturing and assembly facilities (p14)
- Jewellery Division grew 33% with 27% same-store growth; Watches grew 17% (p154-155)
- CSR spend ₹90 crore reaching 9.8 lakh+ beneficiaries across 66 social intervention areas (p13)
