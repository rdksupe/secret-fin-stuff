# TITAN — promised vs delivered

**Mixed credibility.** Management's growth and strategic promises (Q3 acceleration, Damas consolidation, Q1 FY27 ticket-size benefit) were largely delivered with figures to show. Margin commitments were only partly kept—CaratLane stayed just below double digits and jewellery hovered near 11%—and store-count targets could not be verified from later calls.
