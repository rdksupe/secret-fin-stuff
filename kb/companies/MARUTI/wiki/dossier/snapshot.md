# MARUTI — what the business is

Maruti Suzuki India Limited (MSIL) is primarily in the business of manufacturing, purchase and sale of motor vehicles, components and spare parts, with other activities including engineering and ancillary services, pre-owned car sales facilitation, fleet management and car financing. It offers a wide passenger vehicle portfolio in India (hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, MPVs, vans) across petrol, mild hybrid, strong hybrid, CNG and battery-electric powertrains, and is a major exporter supported by Suzuki Motor Corporation's global network.

**Geographies:** Operations are India-centric with significant exports: FY 2025-26 revenue from operations was ₹1,477,985 million domestic and ₹355,175 million overseas (p255). Overseas sales are primarily to a single customer based in Japan contributing more than 10% of total revenue (p255). Exports were a record 447,774 units, supported by Suzuki Motor Corporation's network in over 120 export markets (p144).

- Total income FY 2025-26: ₹1,876,580 million, up 18.82% from ₹1,579,326 million (p114)
- Profit after tax: ₹144,454 million, up 1.03%; PBT ₹188,629 million, down 2.83% (p114)
- Total sales at all-time high of 2,422,713 units; exports 447,774 units (up 34.63%); domestic sales 1,974,939 units (up 3.85%) (p114, p144)
- Retail market share about 40%; SUVs over 56% of India PV sales; recommended dividend of ₹140 per share amounting to ₹44,016 million (p114, p143-144)
- Operating margin moderated to 8.4% from 10.0%; new Kharkhoda and Hansalpur plants (250,000 units each annually) to commence production in FY 2026-27 (p144)
