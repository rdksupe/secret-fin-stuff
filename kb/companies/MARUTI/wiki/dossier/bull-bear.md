# MARUTI — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Scale and demand remain strong: FY26 sales hit record 2,422,713 units, domestic +3.85%, exports +34.63%, retail share ~40%. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p114, p143-144)
- Revenue momentum is robust: total income rose 18.82% to ₹1,876,580m; recent quarterly revenue growth reached 26-36% YoY. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p114; quarterly_actuals 2025-12-31 to 2026-09-30)
- Capacity expansion supports growth: Kharkhoda and Hansalpur add 500,000 units annually from FY27; recommended dividend ₹140/share totaling ₹44,016m. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p114, p144; Q4 FY26 transcript p.4)

## Bear

- Profit quality lagged revenue: FY26 PAT rose only 1.03% while PBT fell 2.83%; operating margin compressed to 8.4% from 10.0%. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p114, p144)
- FY27 deterioration is sharp: op margin fell to 4.6% in Q1 and 5.1% in Q2; Q2 PAT dropped 26.4% YoY. (quarterly_actuals 2026-06-30, 2026-09-30)
- Management’s near-term margin promise missed: expansion within a quarter or two was followed by margins roughly halving; overseas revenue depends on one Japan customer >10%. (promised_vs_delivered Q4 FY26; annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p255)

## Watch next quarter

- Q3 FY27 operating margin: recovery toward 8%+ versus continued 4-5% would validate or break the margin-expansion claim.
- Evidence that Kharkhoda/Hansalpur 500,000-unit capacity is commissioned and FY27 volume growth is tracking above 10%.
- Disclosure on ₹750 crore MTM reversal, FY27 capex of ₹14,000 crore, and export/Japan single-customer concentration.
