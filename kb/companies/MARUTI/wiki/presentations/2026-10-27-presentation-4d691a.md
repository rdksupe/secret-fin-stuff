### Financial Analysis of Q2 FY'27 w.r.t. Q1 FY'27

### **Key reasons for margin movement**

#### **Negative Factors**

- Adverse commodity prices in the context of West Asia conflict
- Adverse foreign exchange movement
- Higher manufacturing overheads
- Higher depreciation expense due to new facility and new model dies
- Lower non-operating income due to mark-to-market losses on invested surplus

#### **Positive Factors**

- Improved realization
- Favourable Fixed Cost Incidence (FCI) due to inventory accretion
- Cost reduction efforts
