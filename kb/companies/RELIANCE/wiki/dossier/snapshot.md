# RELIANCE — what the business is

Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) is a diversified Indian company whose portfolio spans Digital Services (Jio telecom/digital), Retail, Media & Entertainment, Oil to Chemicals (O2C), Oil and Gas, and New Energy businesses. It delivered record consolidated revenue of ₹11,75,919 crore (US$ 124.0 billion) and EBITDA of ₹2,07,911 crore (US$ 21.9 billion) in FY 2025-26, driven by double-digit growth in Digital Services, Retail and Media & Entertainment.

| Segment | Share | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Digital Services (Jio) | not stated | Gross revenue ₹1,76,164 crore; EBITDA ₹76,560 crore, up 17.8% Y-o-Y; 268 million 5G subscribers; ~13 million JioAirFiber premises |
| Retail | not stated | Gross revenue ₹3,71,085 crore, up 12.1%; EBITDA ₹27,034 crore, up 7.7%; 387 million registered customers; 1.9 billion+ annual transactions |
| Media & Entertainment | not stated | EBITDA up 218.7% with record viewership and double-digit revenue growth |
| Oil to Chemicals (O2C) | not stated | EBITDA up 10.1% via higher operating rates, optimised feedstock sourcing and higher domestic placements |
| Oil and Gas | not stated | EBITDA down 10.1% on natural decline in KG D6 production and softer gas prices |

**Geographies:** Primarily India-based operations with exports of ₹2,78,808 crore; subsidiaries span Singapore, USA, Malaysia, Middle East (DMCC) and other international locations (related-party disclosures).

- Consolidated revenue ₹11,75,919 crore (US$ 124.0 billion), up 9.8% Y-o-Y
- EBITDA ₹2,07,911 crore (US$ 21.9 billion), up 13.4% Y-o-Y; PAT ₹95,754 crore (US$ 10.1 billion), up 17.8%
- Net debt ₹1,24,717 crore (US$ 13.2 billion); gross debt ₹3,74,421 crore as of March 31, 2026
- Capex ₹1,44,271 crore (US$ 15.2 billion), funded by cash profits, directed to O2C, New Energy, Digital Services and Retail
- Recommended dividend of ₹6 per share (₹8,119 crore); JV with Meta (₹855 crore, 70:30) for enterprise AI solutions
