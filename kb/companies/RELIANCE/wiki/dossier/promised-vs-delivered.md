# RELIANCE — promised vs delivered

**Mixed credibility.** Credibility is mixed: near-term momentum and Jio margin/FMCG scale claims were broadly borne out, but several dated operational targets are not explicitly confirmed later. Reported figures are mostly consistent, though PAT volatility in Q4 FY26 (-12.6%) and Q1 FY27 (-22.4%) tempers a clean high score.
