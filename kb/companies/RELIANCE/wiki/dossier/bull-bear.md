# RELIANCE — bull vs bear

## Bull

- FY26 revenue ₹11,75,919 crore (+9.8%), EBITDA ₹2,07,911 crore (+13.4%), PAT ₹95,754 crore (+17.8%); Q2 FY27 revenue +28.6%, PAT +40.6%. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p.44; quarterly_actuals 2026-09-30)
- Jio EBITDA ₹76,560 crore (+17.8%) with 268 million 5G subscribers; Retail gross revenue ₹3,71,085 crore (+12.1%), 387 million registered customers. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p.80)
- Net debt ₹1,24,717 crore versus ₹2,07,911 crore EBITDA; ₹1,44,271 crore capex was funded by cash profits; dividend ₹6/share. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p.100)

## Bear

- PAT is volatile: Q4 FY26 fell 12.6% and Q1 FY27 fell 22.4% despite revenue growth, before Q2 FY27 rebounded 40.6%. (quarterly_actuals 2026-03-31; 2026-06-30; 2026-09-30)
- Margins compressed into FY27: Q4 FY26 EBITDA margin 14.8%/operating 9.8%, Q1 FY27 15.2%/10.3%, below Q2 FY27 16.9%/12.3%. (quarterly_actuals 2026-03-31; 2026-06-30; 2026-09-30)
- Gross debt ₹3,74,421 crore and capex ₹1,44,271 crore are sizable; Oil & Gas EBITDA fell 10.1% on KG D6 decline and softer gas. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p.44; p.100)

## Watch next quarter

- Whether Q2 FY27 PAT +40.6% and 16.9%/12.3% margins hold after Q4 FY26/Q1 FY27 PAT declines.
- Jio ARPU ₹215.6 and 53.3% margin trend; Retail online positive unit economics remain not stated.
- O2C cracks versus Oil & Gas KG D6 decline, alongside ₹1,44,271 crore capex and net debt trajectory.
