# SUNPHARMA — what the business is

Sun Pharma is a global pharmaceutical company with a diversified presence across innovative medicines, branded generics, generics and active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), serving patients in more than 100 countries. It also has a consumer healthcare business and focuses on specialty segments of dermatology, ophthalmology and onco-dermatology.

| Segment | Share | Note |
|---|---|---|
| India Formulations | ~33-34% of consolidated revenues | FY26 revenue ₹193 Billion, grew 14%; market share 8.4% (Pharmarack MAT March 2026); largest pharma company in India |
| US | 29% of consolidated revenues | FY26 revenue ₹168.2 Billion, up 3.6%; 13th largest generics company in the US; Innovative Medicines exceeded generics share of US sales for the first time |
| Emerging Markets | 19% revenue share | FY26 revenue ₹111,865 Million, grew 18.8%; presence in 80+ countries; 3,300+ sales reps; manufacturing in 9 markets |

| API | 4% revenue share | FY26 revenue ₹21,853 Million, up 2.6%; ~400 APIs; 13 manufacturing units; 409 DMF/CEP approvals |
| Global Consumer Healthcare | not stated | 25+ countries; top-10 consumer healthcare company in India, Romania, Nigeria, Myanmar; brands Revital, Volini, Prohance |

**Geographies:** Serves 100+ countries; 80+ emerging markets with established operations; international markets contribute 66% of revenues and India 34%; manufacturing footprint of 40 facilities globally including in Bangladesh, South Africa, Malaysia, Romania, Egypt, Morocco, Nigeria and Russia.

- FY26 consolidated revenue from operations ₹584,620.4 Million, up 11.9% (gross sales ₹582 Billion)
- FY26 EBITDA ₹177 Billion, up 16.1%; net profit ₹115,645.2 Million, up 5.0%
- R&D spend ₹35,540 Million, 6.1% of sales; 6 global R&D centres; 2,700+ R&D professionals
- Agreement to acquire Organon at US$14.00/share, enterprise valuation US$11.75 Billion, enabling entry into biosimilars as a Top 10 global player
- 47,000+ employees; 40 manufacturing facilities; total FY26 dividend ₹16.00 per share
