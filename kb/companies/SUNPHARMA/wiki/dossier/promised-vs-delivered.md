# SUNPHARMA — promised vs delivered

**High credibility.** Management is largely credible: dated commitments—UNLOXCYT launch, ILUMYA sBLA, day-one India semaglutide, FY27 R&D guidance, margin normalization—were met as promised. The one clear miss is the ~27.8% tax-rate assurance, with Q2 FY27 ETR near 37%; FY27 revenue guidance and Organon closing remain open.
