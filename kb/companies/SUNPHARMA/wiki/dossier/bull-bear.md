# SUNPHARMA — bull vs bear

## Bull

- FY26 revenue rose 11.9% to ₹584.6bn and EBITDA 16.1% to ₹177bn, with India +14%, Emerging Markets +18.8% and RoW +19.6%. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md)
- Innovative Medicines reached 22% of sales; in the US, innovative products exceeded generics for the first time, reducing pure-generic dependence. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md)
- Execution credibility is high: UNLOXCYT launch, ILUMYA sBLA filing, semaglutide day-one readiness and margin normalization were delivered. (promised_vs_delivered; Q4 FY26 call p4)

## Bear

- Q2 FY27 PAT fell 36.2% as ETR ran near 37% versus management’s ~27.8% assurance, a clear guidance miss. (quarterly_actuals 2026-09-30; promised_vs_delivered)
- US momentum is soft: FY26 US revenue grew only 3.6%, while US sales fell 1.1% in Q4 FY26 and 9.7% in Q1 FY27. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md; Q4 FY26 p5; Q1 FY27 p5)
- Organon adds US$11.75bn enterprise-value scale and biosimilars entry, but closing remains pending and integration economics are not stated. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md; Q1 FY27 p3, p6)

## Watch next quarter

- Whether ETR moves back toward ~27.8% or stays near Q2 FY27’s ~37%.
- Organon closing by early 2027/Q4 FY27 and any disclosed financing or synergy terms; none are stated.
- US generics compliance/recovery versus continued innovative-mix gains; compliance status is not stated.
