# DIXON — promised vs delivered

**Mixed credibility.** Mixed credibility: segment financial targets (IT hardware, capex, Q1 FY27 volume rebound) were met, but core smartphone volume guidance was repeatedly missed and later revised down. FY26 volumes guided at 40-42M closed at 33M, Q4 guided 7-7.5M came in ~5.6-6M, Q Tech's INR2,000 cr/quarter run rate was far off, and telecom FY27 guidance was cut within one quarter.
