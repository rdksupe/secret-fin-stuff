# DIXON — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Revenue scaled to INR21,793.64 cr in Q2 FY27, +46.7% YoY, after Q1 FY27 +21.1% YoY. (quarterly_actuals/2026-09-30; quarterly_actuals/2026-06-30)
- Q1 FY27 PAT rose 194.9% YoY to INR663.42 cr on INR15,547.66 cr revenue. (quarterly_actuals/2026-06-30)
- Some targets landed: FY26 IT hardware ~INR1,300 cr, H2 capex ~INR508 cr vs ~INR550 cr guide, Q1 FY27 volumes +34% QoQ. (guidance-ledger/Q2 FY26 p.13, p.10; Q4 FY26 p.4; Q1 FY27 p.9, p.20)

## Bear

- Smartphone guidance missed: FY26 volumes 33M vs 40-42M guide, ~22% below; Q4 ~5.6-6M vs 7-7.5M. (guidance-ledger/Q2 FY26 p.8; Q3 FY26 p.7; Q4 FY26 p.17)
- PAT swung from +194.9% YoY in Q1 FY27 to -48.7% in Q2 FY27; Q2 margin fell to 2.7%. (quarterly_actuals/2026-06-30; quarterly_actuals/2026-09-30)
- Q Tech ran ~INR425 cr/qtr versus INR2,000 cr/qtr promise; Longcheer slipped to Q3 FY27; telecom FY27 guide was cut within one quarter. (guidance-ledger/Q3 FY26 p.12; Q4 FY26 p.9, p.5; Q1 FY27 p.20)

## Watch next quarter

- Longcheer JV: whether revised Q3 FY27 operational timeline holds after April 2026 slip.
- Telecom/smartphones: run-rate versus revised INR6,700-7,000 cr FY27 telecom guide and sustained volume recovery after 33M FY26 miss.
