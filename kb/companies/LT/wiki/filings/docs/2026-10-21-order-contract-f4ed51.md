## L&T Wins Orders (\*Large) in New Delhi and Andhra Pradesh

**Mumbai, October 21, 2026:** The Public Spaces, Airports & Health (PAH) business vertical of L&T has secured two important orders in India recently.

Awarded by the Central Public Works Department, the first order pertains to the construction of Government of India's Common Central Secretariat buildings 4 and 5 along Kartavya Path in New Delhi.

To be implemented on an engineering, procurement and construction basis, the project comprises two buildings with a configuration of basement + ground + 7 floors and having a total built-up area of 32.8 lakh sq. ft. L&T's scope encompasses structural, architectural, MEP and external development works. The project is scheduled for completion in 24 months, followed by a five-year operation and maintenance period.

The second order, awarded by the Government of Andhra Pradesh, pertains to the construction of two office towers on either side of the Amaravati Quantum Computing Centre at Amaravati.

The two proposed towers are configured as basement + ground + 47 floors and together cover a built-up area of 37.5 lakh sq. ft. A design & build order, L&T's scope includes shell and core works, with the project scheduled for completion in 30 months.

L&T's PAH business vertical has built an impressive track record of delivering modern and customised public spaces across India and overseas. These latest orders further reinforce the confidence that government agencies place in L&T's ability to undertake demanding projects and deliver them to the highest standards.
