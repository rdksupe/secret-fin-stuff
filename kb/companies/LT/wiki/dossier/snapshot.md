# LT — what the business is

Larsen & Toubro is a diversified engineering, construction and manufacturing conglomerate that executes large, complex projects and manufactures engineered-to-order equipment, alongside technology, financial services and realty businesses. It positions itself as a tech-driven solutions provider, using digitalisation, AI and IoT across its project life-cycle.

| Segment | Share | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Infrastructure & Utilities (Infrastructure Projects) | not stated (FY2025-26 revenue ₹79,785 crore; 57% of order book; 46% of order inflow) | Largest contributor; spans Buildings & Factories, Transportation Infrastructure, Heavy Civil, Power Transmission & Distribution, Water & Effluent Treatment, Minerals & Metals (p35, p44, p45) |
| Energy - Conventional | not stated (Energy Projects segment revenue ₹54,889 crore) | Hydrocarbon Onshore & Offshore and CarbonLite Solutions; order inflows ₹1,36,921 crore, up 56.4%; 74% international revenue; OPM declined to 6.8% (p35, p84) |

**Geographies:** Operations span multiple geographies; international revenue was 54% of Group revenue in FY2025-26 (vs 50% prior year) and international orders 52% of the order book, with a large chunk of order wins from GCC countries (p10, p44).

- Group Revenue of ₹2.86 lakh crore in FY2025-26, up 12% y-o-y (p10)
- Record Order Book of ₹7.40 lakh crore, up 28% y-o-y; international orders 52% (p10)
- Recurring PAT of ₹17,238 crore, up 18% y-o-y (p10)
- Group Net Debt:Equity improved to 0.35:1 in FY2025-26 from 0.6:1 (p10)
- Recommended final dividend of ₹38 per share for FY2025-26 (p11)
