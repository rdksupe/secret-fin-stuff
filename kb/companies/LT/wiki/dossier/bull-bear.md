# LT — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Record ₹7.40 lakh crore order book, up 28% YoY with 52% international orders, gives strong multi-year execution visibility. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p10, p44)
- FY26 delivered ₹2.86 lakh crore revenue (+12%), recurring PAT ₹17,238 crore (+18%), and net debt/equity improved to 0.35:1 from 0.6:1. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p10)
- FY27 started with order inflows of ₹1,080 billion, up 14% YoY, while Q1 revenue rose 7% and PAT rose 14%. (Q1 FY27 call p.4, p.6; quarterly_actuals 2026-06-30)

## Bear

- Management missed FY26 revenue (12% vs 15%), P&M margin (7.8% vs 8.5%) and ROE (15.5% vs 18%) commitments. (Q4 FY26 call p.5, p.7, p.13)
- Margins remain pressured: Energy margin fell to 6.5% vs 8.2%, PPM margin was 7.0% in Q1 FY27, and latest quarterly operating margin is 7.3%. (Q4 FY26 call p.8; Q1 FY27 call p.6; quarterly_actuals 2026-09-30)
- Execution risk is visible: ₹50 billion Q4 revenue lost to Middle East supply-chain issues, Kuwait $4.5 billion tenders cancelled, divestments slipped. (Q4 FY26 call p.14; Q3 FY26 call p.9; Q4 FY26 call p.4)

## Watch next quarter

- Whether FY27 revenue stays within 10-12% guidance after the flagged soft first half.
- P&M and Energy margin recovery toward the missed 8.5% FY26 P&M target.
- Closure of Nabha Power and Hyderabad Metro divestments, plus Kuwait re-tender awards during calendar 2026.
