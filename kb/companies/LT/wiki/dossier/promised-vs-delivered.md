# LT — promised vs delivered

**Mixed credibility.** Mixed credibility: management beat its FY26 order inflow guidance and gave accurate directional margin warnings, but missed its headline FY26 revenue (12% vs 15%), P&M margin (8.5% target) and ROE (15.5% vs 18%) commitments. To its credit, management explicitly acknowledged the revenue and margin misses on the Q4 FY26 call rather than obscuring them.
