# ICICIBANK — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Loan momentum strong: Q2 FY27 advances +30% YoY and +12.3% QoQ (+24% ex-FCNR), building on FY26 loan growth of 15.8% to Rs 15,538.93 billion. (transcripts/2026-10-concall p.3; annual-reports/annual-report-2026 p.103)
- Profit accelerating: Q2 FY27 PAT +22.3% YoY to Rs 16,333 crore, NII +15.9%, fee income +20.1% to Rs 7,795 crore after +23.5% in Q1. (dossier/trajectory; transcripts/2026-10-concall p.3)
- Guidance credibility rated high: promises on NIM stability, profit recovery, 32bps credit cost and no capital raise were all delivered. (guidance-ledger)

## Bear

- NIM compressed to 4.18% in Q2 FY27 from 4.36% in Q1 on FCNR deployment; recovery is only a Q3 FY27 promise, not yet delivered. (transcripts/2026-10-concall p.3, p.5)
- Agri-PSL provision write-back slipped from 'next few months' to 'coming quarter or next', still pending RBI discussion — the main open guidance item. (guidance-ledger; transcripts/2026-10-concall p.9)
- Capital adequacy drifting down: 17.18% in FY26 to 16.84% (Jun-26) and 16.68% (Sep-26); earnings showed volatility with PAT -2.7% YoY in Dec-25 quarter. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026 p.103; transcripts/2026-10-concall p.3; dossier/trajectory)

## Watch next quarter

- Q3 FY27 NIM print: management promised an uptick once the full FCNR impact is reflected — test whether 4.18% was the trough.
- Agri-PSL provision write-back landing in Q3/Q4 FY27 per the revised timeline, contingent on the RBI discussion.
- Durability of ex-FCNR loan growth (+24% YoY vs +30% headline) and whether CAR stabilises after sliding to 16.68%.
