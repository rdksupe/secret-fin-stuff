# ICICIBANK — promised vs delivered

**High credibility.** Credibility is high: near-term promises on NIM stability, profit recovery, credit costs and capital were met, with the Q1 FY27 NIM dip occurring under the FCNR caveat management itself flagged. The main open item is the agri-PSL provision write-back, which slipped from 'next few months' to 'coming quarter or next' and remains pending.
