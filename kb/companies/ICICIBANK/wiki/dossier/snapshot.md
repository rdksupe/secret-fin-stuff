# ICICIBANK — what the business is

ICICI Bank Limited is a banking company whose fiscal 2026 annual report describes a bank offering retail banking, business banking, corporate banking and treasury services through branches, digital platforms (iMobile, InstaBIZ, DigiEase, Retail Net Banking) and a network of 7,511 business centres. Through subsidiaries and associates it also has life insurance, general insurance, securities, home finance, asset management and other financial services businesses, consolidated in its group results.
