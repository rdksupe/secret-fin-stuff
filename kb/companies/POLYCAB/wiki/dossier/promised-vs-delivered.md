# POLYCAB — promised vs delivered

**High credibility.** Management's checkable near-term promises were largely met: Q4 margins improved, price pass-through landed, capex stayed within guidance, and exports rebounded as committed. Deviations were mostly favorable (working capital and W&C margins beat the guided ranges); EPC margins stayed lumpy and long-dated Project Spring targets remain untested.
