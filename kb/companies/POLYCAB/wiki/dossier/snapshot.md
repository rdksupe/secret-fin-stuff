# POLYCAB — what the business is

Polycab India Limited is India's largest electrical industry company by revenue, manufacturing and selling wires & cables (W&C) and fast-moving electrical goods (FMEG) such as switches, switchgear, solar products, fans and lighting. It also runs an EPC business and an international/export business, executing a five-year strategic plan called Project Spring.

| Segment | Share | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Wires & Cables (W&C) | 87% of revenue | Revenue grew 33% YoY to ₹252 billion; EBITDA margin 13.9%; market share ~30-31% in India's organised W&C market |
| FMEG | 7% of revenue | Revenue up 25% YoY to ₹20,693 million; includes switches, switchgear, solar (3.5x volume growth), fans; profitable after Q4FY25 breakeven |
| EPC | 6% of revenue | Revenue ₹16.7 billion, down 13.2% YoY; EBIT ₹1.7 billion at 9.9% margin; ~₹80 billion BharatNet Phase-III contracts secured |

**Geographies:** Primarily India (domestic business), with exports of ₹15,695 million contributing 5.4% of consolidated revenue, growing 16.7% YoY; footprint expanded to 94 countries (from 48 in FY19), with strong performance in the Middle East and Latin America; targeting exports >10% of revenue by FY30.

- Consolidated revenue ₹288,838 million, up 29% YoY; PAT ₹27,084 million, up 32% YoY
- EBITDA grew 35% YoY with margins at 13.9%
- Net cash position of ₹41,940 million; FY26 capex ₹14.8 billion (highest ever); planned capex ₹60–80 billion over five years
- Credit ratings upgraded to CRISIL AAA/Stable and IND AAA/Stable (short-term CRISIL A1+ and IND A1+ reaffirmed)
- Recommended dividend of ₹47 (470%) per ₹10 equity share; Project Spring targets W&C EBITDA margins 11–13% and FMEG margins 8–10%
