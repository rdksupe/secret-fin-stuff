# POLYCAB — bull vs bear

## Bull

- FY26 revenue ₹288,838m (+29%), PAT ₹27,084m (+32%), EBITDA +35% at 13.9% margin; India’s largest electricals player with ~30-31% organised W&C share. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p.7, p.23)
- Momentum accelerated: Q2 FY27 revenue +52.8% YoY, PAT +42.6%, EBITDA margin 14.4%; Q1/Q2 W&C EBIT margins 13.3%/14.2% beat Project Spring’s 11-13%. (quarterly_actuals 2026-09-30; promised_vs_delivered Q1/Q2 FY27)
- Net cash ₹41,940m, CRISIL/IND AAA upgrades, ₹47 dividend; exports ₹15,695m (+16.7%) across 94 countries, targeting >10% of revenue by FY30. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p.7, p.29, p.47)

## Bear

- Concentration is high: W&C is 87% of revenue; EPC fell 13.2% YoY and margins were lumpy at 11% Q1 FY27, 7.2% Q2. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p.23, p.28; promised_vs_delivered EPC)
- Margin risk: W&C EBIT ran above the 11-13% Project Spring band, while Q4 FY26 PAT grew only 6.3% despite revenue +26.9%. (promised_vs_delivered Q1/Q2 FY27; quarterly_actuals 2026-03-31)
- Execution load: FY26 capex ₹14.8bn was highest ever within ₹60-80bn five-year plan; exports are 5.4% versus >10% FY30 goal. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p.7, p.29, p.47)

## Watch next quarter

- Q3 FY27 W&C EBIT margin versus 11-13% Project Spring range and whether commodity price pass-through holds.
- Exports after Q2 FY27’s ~85% QoQ jump: order-book conversion toward >10% of revenue by FY30.
- EPC margin stability/BharatNet execution and working-capital normalization from 11-15 days toward guided 50-55; no post-Q2 FY27 filings provided.
