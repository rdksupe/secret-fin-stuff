# TCS — promised vs delivered

**Mixed credibility.** Mixed credibility: segment and cash-return promises (BFSI, manufacturing, dividends, wage-hike impact) were kept, but the repeated march toward the 26-28% margin band went backwards to 24% and FY26 constant-currency growth declined. Directional optimism on growth proved accurate; margin aspirations did not.
