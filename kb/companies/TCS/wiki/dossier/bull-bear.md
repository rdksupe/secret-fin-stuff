# TCS — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Revenue momentum improved: Q4 FY26 ₹70,698cr (+9.6% YoY), Q1 FY27 +13.9%, Q2 +11.2%; FY26 TCV US$40.7bn with 5 mega deals. (snapshot p9; quarterly_actuals 2026-03-31, 2026-06-30, 2026-09-30)
- Profitability and cash returns remain strong: FY26 operating margin 25.0%, net margin 19.8%, net income ₹52,820cr (+8.8%), dividend ₹110/share. (snapshot p9)
- AI optionality is real: US$2.3bn annualised AI services and US$11.5bn new-age services, backed by 580,000 consultants across 56 countries. (snapshot p9; snapshot what_it_does)

## Bear

- Underlying demand weaker than reported: FY26 constant-currency revenue fell 2.4% YoY while reported grew 4.6%; developed-market growth promise was missed. (promised_vs_delivered Q4 FY26 p7; snapshot p9)
- Margin credibility strained: op margin slid from 25.3% Q4 FY26 to 24.0% in Q1/Q2 FY27; wage hikes cut 170bps, 26-28% band unmet. (quarterly_actuals 2026-03-31, 2026-06-30, 2026-09-30; promised_vs_delivered Q1 FY27 p3)
- Earnings quality uneven: Q3 FY26 PAT fell 13.9% YoY despite +4.9% revenue; Q2 FY27 PAT rose 15% but margin stayed 24%. (quarterly_actuals 2025-12-31, 2026-09-30)

## Watch next quarter

- Q3 FY27 operating margin: whether H2 inch-up and FY27 25%+ EBIT exit promise starts delivering.
- Constant-currency international growth, especially BFSI and manufacturing sustaining Q2 FY27 momentum.
- TCV/mega-deal conversion into revenue and evidence AI annualised revenue is scaling beyond US$2.3bn.
