# TCS — what the business is

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), founded in 1968 and part of the Tata Group, is an IT services, consulting and business solutions organisation that partners with many of the world's largest businesses on transformation journeys, offering application development and management, digital transformation, AI, data and cloud services, engineering services, cognitive business operations, cyber security, and products & platforms. With over 580,000 consultants across 56 countries and 194 service delivery centres, it has set an aspiration to become the world's largest AI-led technology services company, positioning itself as a full-stack AI services player from infrastructure to intelligence, and generated consolidated revenues of US$30 billion (₹267,021 crore) in fiscal 2026.

**Geographies:** TCS has an extensive footprint spanning North America, Latin America, the United Kingdom, Continental Europe, Asia Pacific, India and Middle East Africa (p55). Standalone FY2026 revenue by customer location: Americas ₹1,21,267 crore (including US ₹1,16,393 crore), Europe ₹64,648 crore (including UK ₹40,613 crore), India ₹15,101 crore, Others ₹19,922 crore (p293).

- Consolidated FY2026 revenue of ₹267,021 crore (US$30 billion), growth of 4.6% over the previous year (p9, p36)
- Operating margin of 25.0% and net margin of 19.8%, both the highest in the last four years; net income ₹52,820 crore, up 8.8% (p9)
- Total contract value of US$40.7 billion, with 5 mega deals signed during the year (p6, p9)
- Annualised revenues of US$2.3 billion in AI services and US$11.5 billion in new-age services (Cloud, Data, Enterprise Solutions, Cyber Security) (p9)
- Total dividend of ₹110 per share for FY2026, including a recommended final dividend of ₹31 per share (p9)
