# BHARTIARTL — what the business is

Bharti Airtel is the world's second-largest telecom operator by customer base, serving nearly 666 million customers globally through an integrated digital network and a diversified portfolio of services. Its integrated portfolio brings together mobility, broadband, digital TV, and enterprise solutions including data centres, cloud, cyber-security and digital platforms.

**Geographies:** Presence in 17 countries (including Bangladesh and Sri Lanka through associate entities); #1 integrated communications service provider in India and #2 mobile operator in Africa; the Airtel Africa Foundation operates across 14 countries.
