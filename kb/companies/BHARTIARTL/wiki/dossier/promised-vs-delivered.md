# BHARTIARTL — promised vs delivered

**High credibility.** Management delivered on every near-term checkable promise: cloud deals, elevated capex, dividend progression, B2B growth, ARPU gains and the Africa stake swap. The large data center targets (1 GW, 25% share, 56 edge sites) and Airtel Money listing remain untested, and Nxtra's AI efficiency targets were never revisited.
