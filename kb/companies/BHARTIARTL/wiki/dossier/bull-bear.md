# BHARTIARTL — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Revenue reached 58,539.1 in Q1 FY27, up 18.4% YoY, with EBITDA margin 56.7% and operating margin 32.4%. (quarterly_actuals/2026-06-30)
- Execution score high: 24 FY26 cloud deals, dividend ₹24 vs ₹16, Africa stake above 79%, B2B up 12%, ARPU ₹264. (promised_vs_delivered; Q1 FY27 p.3-4)
- Homes is scaling fast: revenue ₹77,747 Mn up 31.7% YoY, EBITDA up 32.1%, FWA across 3,300+ cities. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p.40)

## Bear

- Profit is volatile despite growth: PAT fell 55.1% YoY in Q3 FY26 and 33.5% in Q4 FY26, while PBT slipped to 9,960.3. (quarterly_actuals/2025-12-31; 2026-03-31)
- Capex nearly doubled to ₹13,390 Cr in Q1 FY27 from ₹7,200 Cr; free-cash-flow impact is not stated. (Q3 FY26 p.3; Q1 FY27 p.3)
- Big optionality remains unproven: 1 GW, 25% data-center share, 56 edge sites and Airtel Money listing lack later progress; Nxtra AI metrics unrevisited. (promised_vs_delivered)

## Watch next quarter

- Q2 FY27 revenue/PAT: whether 15-28% revenue growth converts to positive PAT after two negative-YoY quarters.
- Capex versus cash generation as home-pass/5G FWA rollout stays elevated; FCF not stated.
- Concrete Nxtra/edge data-center, Google cloud and Airtel Money listing milestones versus stated timelines.
