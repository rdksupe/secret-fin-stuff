# HDFCBANK — promised vs delivered

**Mixed credibility.** Management delivered on its checkable near-term promises — customer acquisition, MSME/mid-market growth, deposit momentum — with strong Q2 FY27 numbers, and operations held steady through the chairman's exit. However, most strategic pledges (margins, CASA, pre-merger growth) are long-dated and untested, and the material does not confirm chairman succession or the CEO strategy unveil.
