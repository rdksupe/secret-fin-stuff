# HDFCBANK — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Profit engine is large and accelerating: FY26 PAT ₹74,671 crore; Q2FY27 revenue +10.5% YoY, deposits +18.8%, AUM +15.3%. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p259; promised_vs_delivered Q2 FY27 reported p.3)
- Near-term promises landed: 1.7M new liability relationships (+25% vs Q1), MSME loans +23.5% YoY, business banking +28%. (promised_vs_delivered; Q2 FY27 reported p.3-4)
- Resilient balance sheet: GNPA 1.15%, total capital 19.71%, Tier 1 17.73%; 98% digital transactions aid efficiency. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p426, p259, p33)

## Bear

- Earnings quality is uneven: Q1FY27 PAT fell 1.3% YoY; Q2FY27 PAT growth slowed to 5.6% despite revenue +10.5%. (quarterly_actuals 2025-06-30, 2026-09-30)
- Core strategic pledges remain untested: pre-merger growth, FY27 margin improvement, CASA toward 38%, and Q3FY27 margin upturn are all 'too early'. (promised_vs_delivered; Q1 FY27 reported p.11; Q2 FY27 reported p.7)
- Governance visibility is incomplete: material does not confirm permanent chairman succession within three months or the new CEO's strategy unveil. (promised_vs_delivered rows marked not measurable)

## Watch next quarter

- Q3FY27 NIM and cost-of-funds trend versus promised margin upturn from Q3 FY27.
- Whether CASA ratio is restated and moves from 34% toward the ~38% pre-merger vision.
- Confirmation of permanent chairman, CEO strategy unveil, and measurable pre-merger growth trajectory.
