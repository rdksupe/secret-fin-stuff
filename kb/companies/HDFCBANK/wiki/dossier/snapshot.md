# HDFCBANK — what the business is

HDFC Bank Limited is a bank delivering financial solutions to large corporates, mid-market companies, MSMEs and retail customers, combining a strong portfolio with customer insights. Its offerings span loans and deposits, investment banking, and other banking products and services such as cash management, trade finance, treasury, forex and derivatives.
