# Anup Bagchi

**MD & CEO (incoming)** at HDFCBANK (appointed)

ICICI Group member since 1992 across treasury, retail, wholesale and investment banking. Executive Director of ICICI Bank 2017–2023 (retail, then wholesale banking), earlier MD & CEO of ICICI Securities, and since June 2023 MD & CEO of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance, where APE crossed Rs 10,407 Cr in FY2025. IIT Kanpur engineering and IIM Bangalore management graduate; Distinguished Alumnus Award, IIT Kanpur 2023.
