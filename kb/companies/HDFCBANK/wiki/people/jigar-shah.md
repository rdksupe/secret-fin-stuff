# Jigar Shah

**Group General Counsel (incoming)** at HDFCBANK (appointed)

Over three decades in legal, compliance and governance in financial services. Managing Director at KKR India (Head of Legal & Compliance, India Leadership Team; Whole-Time Executive Director of KKR India Financial Services), before that MD and Head of Legal – India at JPMorgan Chase, with senior legal roles at Deutsche Bank across India, Hong Kong and London after starting at UK law firms. LLB (Hons) from the University of Leicester; Solicitor in England and Wales.
