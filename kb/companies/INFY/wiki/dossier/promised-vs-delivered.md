# INFY — promised vs delivered

**Mixed credibility.** Mixed credibility: margin guidance (20-22%) has been consistently held and AI revenue growth promises were exceeded, but FY27 revenue guidance was cut twice within two quarters (1.5-3.5% to 1-2%). Management is reliable on margins and AI traction, less so on topline forecasting.
