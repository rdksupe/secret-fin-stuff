# INFY — what the business is

Infosys Limited provides AI-first business consulting and technology services, enabling organizations to unlock AI value at scale through its AI-first value framework, deep domain expertise, and orchestration of an AI-native partner ecosystem. With over four decades of experience, it helps clients ideate, plan and execute their journey to an AI-first future, supported by services brands like Infosys Topaz (AI), Cobalt (cloud) and Aster (marketing).
