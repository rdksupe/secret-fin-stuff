# INFY — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Revenue momentum accelerating: Q1 FY27 +14.0% YoY, Q2 FY27 +10.7%; FY26 revenue ₹1,78,650 crore (US$20.16bn), +9.6% YoY, crossing US$20 billion. (dossier/trajectory; annual-reports/annual-report-2026)
- AI revenue scaling ahead of promise: AI-first share rose from 5.5% (Q3 FY26) to 10.5% (Q2 FY27), +29% QoQ; large deal TCV US$14.9 billion. (guidance-ledger (2026-02 p10; 2026-10 p4))
- Strong cash and returns: FCF ₹33,097 crore with 112.3% conversion of net profit; over US$4 billion returned via dividends and ₹18,000 crore buyback; margin band 20-22% held. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026; quarterly_actuals 2026-06, 2026-09)

## Bear

- FY27 revenue guidance cut twice within two quarters: 1.5-3.5% CC to 1.5-3% to 1-2%; original range abandoned, topline forecasting unreliable. (guidance-ledger (2026-04 p4; 2026-07 p6; 2026-10 p4))
- Manufacturing headwind confirmed: offshoring plus exited European manufacturing deal impacted growth guidance by ~2%; segment figures not stated. (guidance-ledger (2026-04 p14; 2026-10 p8))
- Profit volatility: Q3 FY26 PAT fell 2.2% YoY despite +8.9% revenue; Q2 FY27 PAT growth decelerated to 6.2% from 12.3% in Q1; EBITDA margin compressed from 24.2% to 23.4-23.7%. (dossier/trajectory (quarterly_actuals 2025-12, 2026-06, 2026-09))

## Watch next quarter

- Whether FY27 1-2% CC revenue guidance holds or is cut a third time at the Q3 FY27 call.
- AI revenue share trajectory beyond 10.5% and whether the mega deal delivers >1% growth contribution in FY28 as promised.
- H2 compensation hikes (October/January) impact on the 20-22% margin band, and progress toward ~20,000 graduate hires (8,000 done by Q2).
