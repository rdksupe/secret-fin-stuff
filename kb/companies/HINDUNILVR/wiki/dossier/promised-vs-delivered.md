# HINDUNILVR — promised vs delivered

**High credibility.** Credibility is high: management's H2 FY26 recovery call, Ice Cream demerger timeline, and 22.5-23.5% margin guidance were all met through FY26. Only blemish is Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin of 22.4% (actuals), marginally below the guided band; FY27 growth promises remain unproven but are tracking well.
