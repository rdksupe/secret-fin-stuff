# HINDUNILVR — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Scale and mix: 50 brands, 9M+ outlets, 90% household reach; Home Care ₹23,672 Cr anchors ₹63,763 Cr FY26 turnover. (annual-report-2026 p6-7)
- Profit engine: FY26 EBITDA margin 23.6%, PAT ₹10,652 Cr, ROCE 110.9%, operating cash flow ₹15,839 Cr funding ₹41 dividend. (annual-report-2026 p13-14, p44)
- Momentum/credibility: H2 FY26 recovery delivered; Q2 FY27 revenue +12.2% YoY after Q1 USG 10%, with guidance largely met. (promised_vs_delivered; quarterly_actuals 2026-09-30)

## Bear

- Margin slippage: Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin 22.4% actual, below 22.5-23.5% guided band; Q1 was 22.8%. (quarterly_actuals Jun/Sep-2026; promised_vs_delivered)
- PAT quality uneven: Dec-2025 PAT ₹6,607 Cr (+121%) coincided with demerger one-off; Sep-2026 PAT grew only 3.9% despite +12.2% revenue. (quarterly_actuals Dec-2025/Sep-2026; promised_vs_delivered)
- Growth was modest before FY27: FY26 USG 5%; four quarters to Mar-2025 revenue stayed ₹15.7-15.9k Cr as margins eased 23.8%→23.1%. (annual-report-2026 p13; quarterly_actuals 2024-06 to 2025-03)

## Watch next quarter

- Whether Q3 FY27 EBITDA margin returns to the 22.5-23.5% band after Q2's 22.4%.
- If revenue growth stays double-digit with healthy UVG/USG balance after Q1 USG 10% and Q2 revenue +12.2%.
- One-off/demerger effects washing out of PAT and any update on the FY27 'better than FY26' growth promise.
