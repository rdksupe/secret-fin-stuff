# HINDUNILVR — what the business is

Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL) is India's largest FMCG company, serving consumers for over 90 years with a portfolio of 50 brands across 15 categories spanning Home Care, Beauty & Wellbeing, Personal Care and Foods. It reaches 9 out of 10 Indian households through a distribution network of over 9 million retail outlets, supported by R&D, AI-enabled operations and a large manufacturing footprint.
