CONSOLIDATED RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED 30th SEPTEMBER 2026 
 
 
 
Mumbai, 28th October 2026: Hindustan Unilever Limited announced its results for the quarter ended 30th September 2026. 
Key highlights: 
• Delivered Turnover of ₹ 18,071 crores with USG1 of 15%. Underlying Volume Growth2 was strong at 7%. 
• This marks our highest Underlying Sales Growth in the last 14 quarters. 
• EBITDA grew 8% year-on-year to ₹ 4,089 crores. EBITDA Margin at 22.6% was within the guided range.  
• Profit After Tax before exceptional items grew 9%  year-on-year reflecting our focus on competitive,  volume-led profit 
growth. Reported Profit After Tax at ₹ 2,796 crores was flat due to one-off tax related gains of ₹ 273 crores in exceptional 
items in base period. 
• First half recorded Turnover of ₹ 35,255 crores with USG of 12% and UVG of 6%. EBITDA grew by 8% while PAT (bei)3 grew 
by 9% year-on-year.  
• The Board of Directors declared an interim dividend of ₹ 19 per share for financial year ending 31st March 2027. 
 
September Quarter segment highlights: 
Home Care | Delivered 22% USG, led by double-digit UVG. Competitive, broad-based growth across formats  
Home Care delivered a strong performance  with 22% USG driven by double- digit UVG. Both Fabric Wash and Household Care 
delivered double-digit, broad-based growth across brands and formats. Liquids continued robust double-digit growth trajectory. 
Focused market development and calibrated pricing actions boosted penetration and supported volume resilience . 
Premiumisation in powders through Surf excel Easy Wash Rs. 99 pack continued  to scale well. Consistent strong growth resulted 
in further strengthening  of our market leadership. During the quarter, Surf  excel and Rin liquids enhanced their propositions to 
cater to emerging consumer needs. 
Beauty & Wellbeing | Recorded 16% USG driven by double-digit UVG. Premium Skin Care and Hair Care continued to outperform 
Beauty & Wellbeing continued to step-up performance delivering 16% USG, driven by double-digit UVG. Hair Care accelerated its 
double-digit, volume- led growth  trajectory. Performance was broad -based across formats with P remium Hair Care and 
innovations continuing to outperform. Skin Care and Colour Cosmetics delivered double-digit, volume-led growth. Premium Skin 
Care sustained its growth trajectory driven by double-digit growth in organised trade. During the quarter, we expanded portfolio 
of Simple with new Barrier Repair Range and relaunched Vaseline body lotion with upgraded packaging and proposition. Further, 
we launched premium Body Serum lotions under Dove and expanded Minimalist range in Skin Care and Hair Care,  strengthening 
our presence in evolving consumer demand spaces. 
Personal Care | Reported 9% USG, driven by double-digit USG in Skin Cleansing 
Personal Care delivered 9% USG . Skin Cleansing delivered double-digit USG. Focused market development actions in Pears and 
Dove Bars supported deliver y of double-digit, volume-led growth. Bodywash continued to strengthen penetration and market 
leadership through step- up in sampling initiatives and in -store activations. Oral Care delivered low -single digit USG  driven by 
premium innovations which continued to scale well. During the quarter, Lux Bar was relaunched with contemporary packaging, 
enhancing appeal and reinforcing brand’s beauty positioning. 
Foods | Achieved 9% USG, led by double-digit USG in Lifestyle Nutrition and Packaged Foods 
Foods delivered 9% USG  with low-single digit UVG.  Lifestyle Nutrition a ccelerated its double- digit growth momentum with

Priya Nair, CEO and Managing Director, commented , “In September quarter, we delivered 15% Underlying Sales Growth, our 
highest in the last 14 quarters, with T urnover of ₹ 18,071 crores. Underlying Volume Growth was strong at 7%  reflecting sustained 
momentum driven by our focus on crafting desirable brands and strengthening future-fit Go-to-Market. Indian economy remains 
resilient, with healthy macro indicators  supporting a positive consumption environment. We continue to invest in building long -
term capabilities and remain anchored to the ‘Winning in New India’ strategy of delivering competitive, volume-led profit growth.”
