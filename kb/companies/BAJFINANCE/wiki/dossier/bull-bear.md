# BAJFINANCE — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Profit momentum is strong: Q2 FY27 PAT ₹6,266 cr, +28.5% YoY after Q1 +27.4%; FY26 Q4 was +22.0%. (quarterly_actuals)
- Execution credibility high: H1 FY27 credit cost 1.54-1.56%, ROA 4.6-4.7%, ROE 20.4-20.6% met/beat FY27 guidance ranges. (transcripts/2026-10-23 p3-4; promised_vs_delivered)
- Growth raised: Q2 FY27 AUM +26.5% to ₹5,84,624 cr; FY27 AUM guidance lifted to 26-27%, customer target to 18-20 mn. (transcripts/2026-10-23 p3, p4, p11)

## Bear

- MSME is the clear miss: promised double-digit growth by Q2-Q3 FY27, but Q2 FY27 grew only +3% after Q4 FY26 +6%. (transcripts/2026-04-concall.md p3; transcripts/2026-10-23 p3)
- Earnings are not linear: Q3 FY26 PAT fell 6.3% YoY to ₹3,978 cr, showing downside despite the FY27 rebound. (quarterly_actuals)
- Balance-sheet reliance is heavy: borrowing/security powers proposed to ₹550,000 cr, plus Bajaj Housing Finance RPTs approved up to ₹18,152 cr. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p3-4)

## Watch next quarter

- MSME growth: whether it moves from +3% in Q2 FY27 back to the promised double-digit trajectory.
- Full-year FY27 delivery versus credit cost 145-160 bps, ROA 4.4-4.6%, ROE 19-20%, and raised AUM/customer guidance.
- Gold loan scale-up toward ~5% of AUM and ₹29,000-31,000 cr, plus evidence of 25-40 bps opex-to-NTI improvement.
