# BAJFINANCE — what the business is

Bajaj Finance Limited (BFL) is a listed Indian financial services company whose annual report is for FY2026 (year ended 31 March 2026). The provided pages are the AGM notice and related shareholder communications; they indicate the company is a lender that borrows from banks, financial institutions and via debentures/bonds/commercial papers and fixed/corporate deposits, and that Bajaj Housing Finance Limited is its subsidiary.
