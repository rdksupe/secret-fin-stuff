# PERSISTENT — bull vs bear

## Bull

- FY26 revenue $1,654.4M (+17.4% YoY) and PAT ₹18,651.2M (+33.21%) with 12.65% margin and 29.27% ROCE show profitable scale. (annual-report-2026 pp.10,12,72)
- Growth accelerated: revenue YoY rose from 21.8% (Jun-25) to 30.3% (Sep-26); Q2 FY27 $487.6M annualises near $1.95bn, close to $2bn target. (quarterly_actuals; promised_vs_delivered)
- Demand indicators are solid: FY26 ACV $1,780.1M, Q4 ACV $445.1M, 86.99% retention, plus AI platforms SASVA, GenAI Hub, Agent Studio and iAURA. (annual-report-2026 pp.262-265)

## Bear

- Margins deteriorated fast: op margin fell 16.3% (Mar-26) to 10.0% (Sep-26), EBITDA margin 18.9% to 13.6%, and PAT turned -25.4% YoY. (quarterly_actuals 2026-03/2026-09)
- Nagarro is the swing risk: close slipped from Q4 CY26 to Q4-CY26/Q1-CY27, Austria/Spain approvals pending, with 5-6 months integration and synergies still unproven. (promised_vs_delivered; transcript pp.6-7,19)
- Management’s 16-17% margin aspiration and fading 90bps large-deal drag remain unproven against Q2 FY27 EBIT 15% and reported 10.0% op margin. (promised_vs_delivered pp.16,18; quarterly_actuals 2026-09)

## Watch next quarter

- Nagarro approvals, close timing, integration costs and first measurable revenue/synergy contribution.
- Whether op margin recovers from 10.0% toward 15-17% as the 90bps large-deal drag fades and PAT growth turns positive.
- Quarterly run-rate versus ~$500M Q4 FY27 exit / $2bn ±a quarter, backed by ACV/TCV conversion.
