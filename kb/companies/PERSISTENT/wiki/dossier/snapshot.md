# PERSISTENT — what the business is

Persistent Systems Limited is a global provider of digital engineering and enterprise modernisation services, specialising in AI-driven solutions and software product engineering for industries such as BFSI, Healthcare and Hi-Tech. It builds software products from conception to deployment, modernises core platforms and embeds AI across operations, supported by proprietary platforms such as SASVA, GenAI Hub, Agent Studio and iAURA.
