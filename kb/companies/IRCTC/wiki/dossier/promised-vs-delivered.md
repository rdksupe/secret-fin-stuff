# IRCTC — promised vs delivered

**Mixed credibility.** Mixed credibility: management delivered on catering growth, Rail Neer plant sanctioning, and the RBI license submission, but overpromised on FY26 revenue growth (12% vs 15% target) and margins, which fell below 30% in Q1 FY27. Tourism and ticketing growth also ran below the ~20% and ~7% targets set on the Q4 FY26 call.
