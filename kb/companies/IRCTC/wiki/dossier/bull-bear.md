# IRCTC — bull vs bear

## Bull

- Online rail ticketing dominance: 88.74% reserved tickets online; 5,304.65 lakh e-tickets +4.68%, 9,308.14 lakh passengers +4.46%. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p12)
- FY26 revenue ₹5,214.86 crore, EBITDA ₹1,926.12 crore, PAT ₹1,393.37 crore; dividend ₹720 crore, EPS ₹17.42. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p5)
- Catering accelerated Q1 FY27 +33.82% YoY to ₹732 crore versus ~15% target. (transcripts/Q1-FY27-concall p4)

## Bear

- Guidance credibility mixed: FY26 revenue grew ~12% versus 15% target; Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin 28.2% versus 30% commitment. (guidance-ledger: Q4 FY26 p3/p13; Q1 FY27 p4)
- Margin pressure: EBITDA margin 35.3% Q2 FY26 to 27.3% Q4 FY26, 28.2% Q1 FY27. (dossier/trajectory)
- Rising revenue share to Ministry ₹858.01 crore FY26 vs ₹638.50 crore FY25; tourism/ticketing below targets. (annual-reports/annual-report-2026.md p5; transcripts/Q1-FY27-concall p4)

## Watch next quarter

- RBI payment-aggregator response expected this financial year; approval or delay changes payments optionality.
- Whether EBITDA margin recovers toward 30% after 28.2% in Q1 FY27.
- Execution on Ambernath/Danapur capacity and four sanctioned Rail Neer plants; tourism growth versus ~20% target.
