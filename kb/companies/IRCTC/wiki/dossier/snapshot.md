# IRCTC — what the business is

IRCTC is a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Railways, Government of India, serving as the hospitality, tourism and online ticketing arm of Indian Railways. It integrates digital ticketing, catering, tourism and packaged drinking water (Rail Neer) into a comprehensive travel ecosystem for rail passengers.

| Segment | Share | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Internet Ticketing | not stated | Digital gateway for rail reservations; 88.74% of reserved tickets booked online (Q2 FY26); 5,304.65 lakh e-tickets booked in FY 2025-26; ₹74,834.62 crore ticket fare collected |
| Catering and Hospitality | not stated | Mobile, static and e-catering across 1,450+ trains, 420+ stations under e-Catering, 800+ AI-monitored kitchens |
| Travel and Tourism | not stated | Leisure, pilgrimage, luxury (Maharajas' Express with 43 luxury cabins) and international tourism; tourism portal gross revenue ₹699.55 crore in FY 2025-26 |
| Rail Neer (Packaged Drinking Water) | not stated | 20 plants, average sales of 12.80 lakh bottles per day |

**Geographies:** India (nationwide rail network coverage); some international tourism offerings and participation in global travel exhibitions (ITB Berlin 2026, IMEX America 2025, WTM London, Luxperience Sydney). Listed on BSE & NSE since 14.10.2019.

- Revenue from operations ₹5,214.86 crore; EBITDA ₹1,926.12 crore; PAT ₹1,393.37 crore; market capitalisation ₹39,552.00 crore as on 31st March 2026
- Total income grew from ₹1,598.71 crore (FY 2016-17) to ₹5,474.97 crore (FY 2025-26); PAT from ₹214.69 crore to ₹1,393.37 crore
- 88.74% share of reserved ticket booking on Indian Railways online; 5,304.65 lakh e-tickets booked (+4.68% YoY); 9,308.14 lakh passengers (+4.46%)
- Revenue share paid to Ministry of Railways: ₹858.01 crore in FY 2025-26 vs ₹638.50 crore in FY 2024-25
- Total dividend declared ₹720 crore in FY 2025-26; EPS ₹17.42; net worth ₹4,308.38 crore
